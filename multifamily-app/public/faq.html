Billing FAQ

Last updated: September 7, 2025

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal from your account page. Your access will continue until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. Please contact support@terra.ai for assistance.

What happens if my payment fails?

Stripe will automatically retry failed payments. If payment cannot be collected, your access may be paused until payment is resolved.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

Yes, you can manage your plan at any time via the Stripe Customer Portal.

How do I get a copy of my invoice?

Invoices and receipts are available in your Stripe Customer Portal.

Who do I contact for billing support?

Email support@terra.ai for any billing questions.
